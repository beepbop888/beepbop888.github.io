# Where the card pictures come from

Every picture in this app — the ones bundled here and the thousands
on `beepbop888.github.io/cards` — is a **public-domain or CC0** work
from a museum that publishes its open-access collection: no
attribution required, free for commercial use. That is the whole
reason these three collections and not stock photography: a greeting
card is forwarded by whoever sends it, into a family chat where there
is nowhere honest to put an attribution string.

Fetched and curated by `python tools/fetch_card_library.py`. The
museum record behind every picture is linked below, and the same
fields travel in the manifest for the pictures that are not bundled.
